# Decision Record Template

Copy to `decisions/NNN-short-title.md` (increment NNN). Keep decisions short and durable.

---

```markdown
# ADR-NNN: <Title>

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Date** | YYYY-MM-DD |
| **Status** | Proposed / Accepted / Superseded |
| **Related stories** | links |

## Context

What forced a decision.

## Decision

What we chose, in one or two sentences.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — pros / cons
- **Option B** — pros / cons

## Consequences

Positive and negative effects on code, ops, and future features.

## Follow-up

Migration steps, docs to update, stories to change.
```
